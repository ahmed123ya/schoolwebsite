About Our Center

A Place Built On Patience, Play & Progress

M&M Montessori & Therapy Center blends authentic Montessori education with evidence-based therapy services, creating one seamless environment where children can build confidence, communication, and independence.

Every classroom and therapy room is designed to feel safe, bright, and welcoming — because we believe children learn best when they feel truly at home.

FAQs

Questions Parents Often Ask

Can't find your answer here? Reach out — we're happy to help.

What age groups do you accept?

We welcome children from 18 months through 12 years, across both our Montessori programs and therapy services.

Do I need a diagnosis before starting therapy?

No — our team begins with a full assessment and can guide you through diagnosis and next steps if needed.

How long is a typical therapy session?

Most sessions run 45–60 minutes, with frequency tailored to your child's personalized plan.

Can parents observe or join sessions?

Absolutely. We encourage parent involvement and offer regular observation and guidance sessions.

Do you offer combined Montessori and therapy plans?

Yes — many of our families combine daily Montessori learning with weekly therapy sessions for consistent, integrated support.
